How do I move my progress to a new iPhone?

Progress syncs automatically through iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices, make sure iCloud is on, and open the game — your stars, coins and badges come with you. You can check the status in the game under Settings → iCloud save.

I'm stuck on a ticket

Tap 💡 Hint — it highlights exactly what to do next. Out of hints? Buy one with coins you earn by resolving tickets.

What is the Daily Bonus?

Open the game once a day and tap 🎁 Daily on the main menu to collect free coins. Come back on consecutive days to grow your streak: day 7 pays the most and includes a free hint. Miss a day and the streak starts over.

How do I start over?

Open Settings (⚙︎) and tap Reset all progress. This erases your progress on this device and in iCloud and can't be undone.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep the game free. You'll see a banner on the tower map and a short ad every few tickets. Watching a video for a free hint, or to double the daily bonus, is always optional.

Privacy

The game itself doesn't collect personal information. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. You control ad tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland can change their ad consent in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
