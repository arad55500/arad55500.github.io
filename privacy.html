Privacy Policy

IT Technician Simulator · Effective November 16, 2026

IT Technician Simulator ("the app") is a free, single-player puzzle game for iPhone made by an independent developer. It works without accounts or sign-ups, and the developer runs no servers for it. This policy explains what information is involved when you play.

Information the developer collects

None. The developer does not collect, receive, store, share or sell personal information from the app. The app has no accounts and no analytics of its own.

Your game progress

  • Your progress (stars, coins, hints, badges, daily-bonus streak and settings) is stored on your device.
  • If you are signed in to iCloud, the same progress is also synced to your own private iCloud account using Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage, so it carries over to your other devices. The developer cannot see or access this data. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • You can erase all progress at any time in the game: Settings → Reset all progress. This removes it from the device and from iCloud.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers: your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; otherwise an identifier specific to this app.
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general (coarse) location.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version.
  • Ad interactions (ads shown, taps, video views) and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information.

Google's use of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The developer does not receive personal information about you from Google; AdMob only gives the developer aggregated reports, such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices

  • Tracking: the app asks for permission in Apple's tracking prompt. You can change your answer at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you don't allow tracking, ads are not personalized using your activity in other companies' apps and websites.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are shown. You can review or change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Optional videos: watching a video for a free hint or to double the daily bonus is always optional.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
